math-by-ai

The source of a mathematics library for Tauto, with each name linked to its definition. GitHub

  • core — logic, order and functions, the rationals, the reals as Dedekind cuts and their uncountability
  • algebra — monoids, groups, semirings and commutative rings, ring_norm!
  • nat — arithmetic and divisibility of the naturals, Euclid's algorithm, Bézout, primes, prime factorization
  • geometry — the plane over an integral domain, the opposite sides of a parallelogram
